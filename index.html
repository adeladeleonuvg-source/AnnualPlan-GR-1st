I) Course Description

This course provides an introduction to the basic elements of English grammar and writing skills. The course also includes active vocabulary that provides students with the necessary language to answer the topic questions that are presented and practiced. Many activities are set up in different ways, according to the needs of students and teachers.

Area Focus: Develop key competencies for the gradual acquisition of English language skills through structured and adaptive learning.

II) General Objectives

Objective 1

Recognition & Identification

Students will be able to recognize and identify vocabulary words within the context of a theme.

Objective 2

Structure & Application

Students will be able to use words with the right structure and apply vocabulary words.

Objective 3

Expression & Introduction

Students will be able to tell self-experiences, recognize new words, give their opinion, and introduce a new topic.

III) Academic Area Competencies

Students develop the fundamental skills for understanding and using English in basic communicative contexts. They demonstrate active listening by following simple instructions and recognizing sounds that differ from their native language.

Learners express themselves through clear pronunciation of familiar words, short phrases, and everyday expressions, supported by appropriate verbal and non-verbal communication.

They begin to read and respond to simple written commands, showing comprehension through actions and responses. These competencies foster the gradual development of oral and written communication skills in English at an introductory level.

IV) Achievement Indicators

✔

Follows simple oral instructions given by the teacher in English.

✔

Recognizes and reproduces basic sounds and words in English with acceptable pronunciation.

✔

Uses common greetings and classroom expressions appropriately in everyday interactions.

✔

Identifies familiar vocabulary through visual aids, songs, and classroom activities.

✔

Demonstrates understanding of simple spoken phrases through physical response or action.

✔

Reads and responds to short written commands and words related to familiar topics.

✔

Combines words or short phrases to form simple and meaningful sentences orally.

✔

Uses gestures and facial expressions to support communication when speaking or listening in English.

V) Institutional Teaching Methods

Active-Collaborative Model

Double Period Structure (70 Minutes)

1. Engaging Warm-up Start

Prior knowledge activation through educational games, energizers, and brainstorming.

2. Individual Work Development

Fostering personal strengths, technical skills, and individual student talents.

3. Teamwork Synergy

Collaborative interaction with assigned roles under the principle: "All for one and one for all".

4. Plenary & Reflection Closure

Public presentation, Socratic dialogue, and learning consolidation.

Key Methodologies & Pedagogical Approaches

Project-Based Learning (PBL) & Case Studies

Utilizing real-life scenarios and collaborative interactions to enhance problem-solving and team synergy.

Constructivism & Flipped Classroom

Empowering students to take ownership of their learning through pre-class reading and proactive reflection.
